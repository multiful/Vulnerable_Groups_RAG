## Anti-Patterns Verdict

**LLM assessment**: `warn` — not an outright fail. The mascot interaction, B12_9 safety gate, localStorage persistence, and domain mismatch warning are thoughtful, non-generic decisions that prove authorial intent. However, several scaffold patterns show through: (1) the PROMISES section uses all-caps English labels (PERSONALIZED, STRUCTURED, EVIDENCE-BACKED) against Korean body copy — a classic mixed-register tell; (2) the home page has 7 sequential sections all following the identical rhythm of `h2 → subtext → grid` with no visual counterpoint; (3) "RAG" appears as a home-page stat tile alongside Korean text without translation; (4) sections "4단계로 완성되는 맞춤 추천" and "추천을 행동으로 바꿉니다" cover near-identical ground consecutively; (5) the hero tagline is repeated verbatim in three separate sections. These are LLM-scaffolding symptoms that weren't sufficiently pruned.

**Deterministic scan**: 3 hits. 2 false positives:
- `chat-bounce` (ChatWidget:202) — name-triggered; actual keyframes are `translateY(0 → -5px → 0)` with default `ease`, no overshoot.
- `mascot-bounce-scale` (Home:247) — name-triggered; easing is `cubic-bezier(0.16,1,0.3,1)` (ease-out-expo), no overshoot.

1 confirmed genuine:
- `transition: width` (Recommendation:2864) — `.outlook-stack-seg` stacked bar segment animates via `width` transition, forcing layout recalculation per frame. This was previously documented as an "architectural exception" but Assessment B correctly identifies it as real layout thrash. The fix involves either removing the transition (instant update) or restructuring the bar to use `clip-path` or absolutely-positioned segments with `transform: scaleX`.

**Visual overlays**: Browser visualization was not attempted; CLI scan results above are the deterministic signal.

## Overall Impression

The technical foundation of this app is solid — the token system, accessibility primitives, and survey safety design are better than most production apps in this domain. But the UX has a fundamental narrative problem: **the app knows the user is vulnerable, does extensive emotional work to gather their situation, and then hands them a clinical welfare label and routes them to a support dashboard before they ever see a career recommendation.** The promise made on the home page ("자격증 추천") is not fulfilled at the moment it should be. For the primary audience — withdrawn youth with low executive function and high rejection sensitivity — this is the gap that matters most.

## What's Working

**1. Survey safety architecture.** The B12_9 pre-question gate ("편하지 않으시면 건너뛰어도 자격증 추천에 전혀 지장이 없습니다"), the `safetyFlag`-triggered banner, and the crisis resources on stage 3-4 results show that someone thought carefully about this population. The skip permission that explicitly anchors the consequence to the user's stated goal ("추천에 전혀 지장이 없습니다") is the single best-written line in the app.

**2. localStorage survey persistence.** 7-day TTL, restore with precise question number, visible reset option — invisible when not needed, obvious when it is. This is exactly right for a population that may abandon and return. Most apps in this space lose survey progress on tab close.

**3. InterestSelection escape design.** The "skip domain" path disappears after selection, the job section is opt-in with a toggle, and the mismatch warning is proactive without blocking. The CTA dynamically reflects the current state ("데이터/AI 로드맵 보기" vs "전체 자격증 추천 보기") — respects user mental model.

## Priority Issues

### [P0] Primary CTA after risk result routes users away from their stated goal

**Why it matters**: A user who disclosed mental health struggles and social isolation to complete a 12-question survey came because they want career guidance. The primary button ("나에게 맞는 지원 보기") routes them to `/isolation/dashboard` — a welfare services page — before they see a single certification card. The cert path ("자격증 추천 받기 →") is a ghost link in a separate DOM block below the main action row. For a user with low energy or low confidence — which is the core audience — this ghost link is invisible. This is the highest drop-off risk in the entire app and a direct breach of the promise made on the home page.

**Fix**: Swap the button hierarchy. Make "자격증 추천 받기" the primary `btn-primary` button, rename it to "내 상황에 맞는 자격증 보기 →". Demote the IsolationDashboard path to a secondary `btn-ghost` or a contextual card embedded within the Recommendation page itself. For stage 3-4 users, a dismissible support card at the top of Recommendation ("이런 상황에 도움이 되는 프로그램도 있어요 ↓") serves both the welfare goal and the career goal without hijacking the primary flow.

**Suggested command**: `/impeccable harden` (structural flow fix) or `/impeccable shape` (redesign the result → recommendation handoff)

### [P1] Stage labels are clinical and identity-threatening at the highest-stakes moment

**Why it matters**: A user who answers 12 questions about their isolation sees "4단계 (은둔 청년)" as the first content rendered on the result screen. This is a taxonomic category from a 2022 welfare research paper designed for caseworkers, not for self-identification. Being labeled with a clinical tier by an algorithm — at a moment of vulnerability, before any warm acknowledgment — is a tab-close risk. The radar chart immediately following is a visual representation of how far their scores are from healthy. Combined: "here is what is wrong with you" rather than "here is where you are starting from."

**Fix**: Remove the tier label from the primary result heading. Use it internally for routing only. Lead instead with a strength-based framing specific to each stage: e.g., stage 4 → "지금 내 상황에서도 시작할 수 있는 자격증들을 찾았어요." If the tier must be shown for transparency (e.g., to explain why certain programs appear), place it in a collapsed detail section below the primary message with a plain-language explanation in a smaller style. The `safetyFlag` result header already shows how to do stage-aware messaging warmly — generalize that pattern.

**Suggested command**: `/impeccable clarify` (rewrite result page copy and heading hierarchy)

---

### [P1] Interest selection forces 43 domains / 142 jobs with no search, no stage-aware pre-filtering

**Why it matters**: After completing a distressing 12-15 question assessment, a stage 3-4 user faces a full taxonomy browser with 9 accordion groups and 12 job groups, all closed by default. There is no text search. There is no indication of which domains tend to work for people at their stage. A withdrawn youth with low executive function will often close the tab here — this is the second-highest drop-off risk after the result CTA issue. The escaped path ("전체 추천 보기") produces an undifferentiated list with no guidance on the cost of skipping.

**Fix**: Add a text search input above the domain groups. Pre-open 2-3 groups statistically associated with the user's stage (a static `stage → [domain_group_ids]` map is sufficient — no backend change needed). Add a one-line note on skipping: "도메인을 선택하면 더 정확한 추천을 받을 수 있어요" with the skip path clearly secondary.

**Suggested command**: `/impeccable adapt` (add search + pre-filter) or `/impeccable craft 관심분야 검색 기능`

---

### [P2] Result page renders 8+ information zones simultaneously — catastrophic density at peak stress

**Why it matters**: The result screen shows simultaneously: stage badge, progress bar, radar chart SVG, 5 category bar rows, AI synthesis card (3 sections), evidence toggle, policy classification, support programs list (multiple grouped items + links), and crisis resources. This is a maximum-density screen at the maximum-stress moment in the flow. A user who answered "거의 매일" to the depression and isolation questions is now reading "공동생활 참여", "사회기술 재학습 프로그램", and "수면 및 위생 관리, 정리정돈". The information is accurate; the presentation is crushing.

**Fix**: Redesign into three phases: (1) immediate reveal — one warm framing line + primary CTA only; (2) on scroll or "내 결과 자세히 보기" — radar chart + category scores + AI synthesis; (3) collapsed card — "이용 가능한 지원 프로그램" expandable, default closed. Crisis resources should be a persistent floating element or dismissible banner, not a document-flow content section.

**Suggested command**: `/impeccable layout` (restructure result page into phased disclosure)

---

### [P2] No closure or affirmation at the end of the 4-step flow

**Why it matters**: Peak-end rule: users disproportionately remember the final moment. After a mental health assessment and career selection flow, the user arrives at a page titled "자격증 확인" with an undifferentiated card list. No acknowledgment that they completed something meaningful. No signal that these results are personalized. For an anxious or withdrawn user, this feels like the app forgot who they are — the emotional work of the survey vanishes.

**Fix**: When Recommendation is reached via the full flow (risk stage param present), inject a single warm header above the cert list: "여기까지 잘 오셨어요. [스테이지 plain-language description] 기준으로 [domain] 분야에서 지금 도전할 수 있는 자격증들을 골랐습니다." One sentence. Zero backend change. The `pipelineState` already carries the stage and domain; this is a conditional render.

**Suggested command**: `/impeccable clarify` (add warm arrival copy to Recommendation page header)

## Persona Red Flags

### Jordan (First-Timer) — 24살, 처음으로 진로 계획을 세우는 사람

- **5-second clarity test: PASS.** Hero CTA "시작하기" is prominent, badge is readable, flow description is clear. This is the strongest first impression.
- **Stage label interpretation failure.** Jordan sees "1단계 (고립위험청년)" with no prior explanation of what this system means. No tooltip, no expandable, no "이게 무슨 뜻이에요?" link. Jordan is expected to interpret a welfare research taxonomy on first encounter.
- **CTA framing mismatch.** The primary result button says "나에게 맞는 지원 보기" — Jordan, who came for career guidance, may interpret "지원" as social welfare services rather than job/cert support.
- **"SCRIPT.md §4" in the precision note.** This internal code reference is visible in the user-facing precision question screen. Jordan will interpret it as a technical glitch, reducing system confidence.
- **Skip consequence unclear.** "일단 전체 추천 보기 →" escape in InterestSelection has no guidance on whether it produces meaningfully worse results. Jordan doesn't know whether spending time selecting a domain is worth it.

### 은둔청년 (Withdrawn Youth) — 22살, 방에서 오래 지냈고 진로 첫 걸음을 내딛으려는 사람

- **The "failure accumulation" survey experience.** Options like "전혀 없다", "전혀 나가지 않는다", "거의 하지 않고 있다" appear across multiple questions with no acknowledgment between them. The user is scrolling through a damage report of their own life, question by question, with no warmth in between.
- **P_Q2 emotional hazard.** "가족 이외에 누군가와 마지막으로 만난 것이 언제입니까?" with option "1달 이상 전" — for a user who hasn't seen a friend in two months, this timestamps their isolation explicitly. The page header at this moment reads "거의 다 됐어요!" — a cheerful tone that collides with the emotional weight of the answer.
- **"4단계 (은둔 청년)" label as first render.** This user experiences shame about their situation. The clinical classification appears before any warm acknowledgment, before the certification recommendations, before any signal that career normalcy is possible.
- **Primary CTA routes them deeper into the isolation infrastructure.** This user came to take a step toward career normalcy. The app's primary response sends them to `/isolation/dashboard` — confirming that they are, in the app's judgment, "an isolation case" rather than "someone starting a career."
- **Predicted tab-close moments:** (a) mid-survey, during the accumulation of "전혀 없다" answers with no acknowledgment; (b) result page, the moment "4단계 (은둔 청년)" renders alongside welfare program listings mentioning hygiene and sleep management.

## Minor Observations

- `💾` emoji in the survey restore bar is a floppy disk — anachronistic for users under 30. Replace with `↩` or text only.
- "RAG", "EVIDENCE-BACKED", "PERSONALIZED", "STRUCTURED" — English terms used as labels inside Korean UI. Either translate or remove; mixed register reads as AI scaffolding.
- Sections "4단계로 완성되는 맞춤 추천" and "추천을 행동으로 바꿉니다" cover near-identical ground on the home page back-to-back. One should be cut or merged.
- "SCRIPT.md §4" visible in user-facing precision note copy. Strip the internal document reference before ship.
- Mobile bottom nav (5 items: 홈, 시험일정, 직업탐색, 진단, 훈련) has no direct path to "자격증 추천" — the primary flow output. A returning user on mobile has no obvious shortcut back to their recommendations.
- The `transition: width` on `.outlook-stack-seg` is confirmed genuine layout thrash (Assessment B). Fix with instant updates (no transition) or a `clip-path` approach for the stacked proportional bar.
- `mascot-bounce-scale` animation has no `@media (prefers-reduced-motion)` in its inline style block. The global kill-switch in `index.css:379` covers it globally, but the absence at the declaration site is a maintenance risk — future editors may not know.
- Home page sticky CTA and mobile bottom nav both fixed simultaneously on mobile — two competing fixed elements below viewport, visually noisy.

## Questions to Consider

1. **Is the IsolationDashboard routing a policy constraint or a flow artifact?** Was routing to `/isolation/dashboard` as the primary post-result CTA a deliberate decision mandated by a welfare partner, or an artifact of development order (IsolationDashboard built before the cert recommendation flow was complete)? Has this been tested with actual users to measure the drop-off rate between result page and first cert card view?

2. **Does the stage label need to be user-visible at all?** The tier taxonomy exists to drive filtering logic (a stage 4 user gets certs from `risk_0004`). Could the filtering run silently and describe the user's situation in plain behavioral language ("지금 집에서 혼자 있는 시간이 많고 사람 만나기가 부담스러운 상황") instead of a clinical category name? This would eliminate the highest emotionally-risky moment in the app with no backend change.

3. **Would staging welfare programs after cert recommendations serve both goals better?** Is there data on whether showing government welfare programs before cert recommendations increases or decreases cert engagement? A post-cert placement — "이런 지원 프로그램도 있어요 ↓" accordion at the bottom of Recommendation — serves both the welfare goal and the career goal without interrupting the primary flow.
